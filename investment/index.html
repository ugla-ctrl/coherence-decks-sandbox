The Place

Carson Creek Ranch.

Held by one family on this river since 1851.
  • 60 acres on the banks of the Colorado River, in Austin, TX
  • A Texas Century Ranch, stewarded by the same family for 150 years
  • Amphitheater Stage on the water, under old pecans: music, yoga, keynotes
  • The Ceremony Garden next to the property's spring for the daily ceremonies and deep practice
  • The Grove: a river trail of food vendors, artisans, biohacking, lounges, and The Bath House
  • Held on the Spring Equinox, through the Full Moon, closing on World Water Day

06 · Place
Investment Deck
COHERENCE
Access

A destination gathering without the journey.

Most transformational gatherings ask for two travel days before anyone arrives. Carson Creek Ranch is 15 minutes from downtown Austin and 5 minutes from Austin-Bergstrom International airport, so a guest can land and be on the land the same hour. Lower friction, higher conversion, national reach.

Zero travel days: fly in the morning, be in ceremony that afternoon. Nonstop flights from both coasts, most under four hours, into the conscious-builder hub itself.

The Four Days

One theme a day. One arc across four.

Friday · Arrival

Opening

Land, find your people in the Grove. Opening Ceremony at 6, dinner, then AWARË opens the music on the river.

Consciousness after the AI Revolution

Daybreaker party at dawn. Keynote & panel on conscious AI and the future of humanity after universal abundance. Future Timeline Ceremony. Poranguí headlines.

Sunday

Consciousness in Financial & Political Systems

Bass Church party at dawn. Keynote & panel: founders, funds and organizers of the new systems of finance and governance. Illuminate the Shadow Systems Ceremony. Snow Raven and MOSE headline.

World Water Day

A slow day to foster connection. The Bath House guided inner-water cleanse. Keynote & panel on the technology of water, the Love Water Ceremony, closing dinner and acoustic dance set.
