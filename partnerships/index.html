Coherence Festival · Partnership deck
Introduction

Where the world's optimists meet, the future gets built.

Coherence is a four-day gathering for the people who believe humanity will triumph through its global challenges: founders, artists, healers, scientists and stewards, on one field, for one long weekend each spring.

What
Part festival, part ceremony, part summit. Four themed days, four ceremonies, three keynote days, six headline music acts.
When
Friday March 19 to Monday March 22, 2027. Spring Equinox through the Full Moon, closing on World Water Day.
Where
Carson Creek Ranch: sixty acres on the Colorado River, fifteen minutes from downtown Austin, five from the airport.
Who
2,500 guests in year one, growing to 10,000 by 2029. Drawn from the Texas Eclipse and Iceland Eclipse communities and Austin's own.
